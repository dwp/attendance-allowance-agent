What is a conflicting benefit?

A conflicting benefit is any payment the claimant may be receiving which could stop them receiving Attendance Allowance.

Conflicting benefits include:

  • Adult Disability Payment (ADP)
  • Disability Living Allowance (DLA)
  • Disability Living Allowance (DLA) 65+
  • Scottish Adult Disability Living Allowance (SADLA)
  • Personal Independence Payment (PIP)
  • Personal Adult Disability Payment (PADP)

If the claimant is under State Pension age, they are not eligible for Attendance Allowance.
